# Psychology and behavioral science

Map each central construct to its operational variable. Record the task or instrument version, respondent, population, language, timing, scoring, transformations, thresholds, exclusions, and evidence offered for validity and reliability. Ask whether the measure supports the precise construct and comparison claimed.

For public preregistrations, resolve the exact OSF registration URL or identifier. Call `lookup_source` with provider `osf`, that identifier, and the manuscript block ID. Distinguish an immutable registration from a mutable project or an uploaded file. Compare hypotheses, outcomes, conditions, sample and stopping plans, exclusions, scoring, confirmatory analyses, and dated amendments. Label deviations by consequence and chronology. An openly labeled exploratory analysis is not a failed confirmatory test.

Inspect randomization, experimental unit, repeated measures, manipulation fidelity, demand characteristics, attrition, exclusions, missing trials, multiple outcomes, researcher degrees of freedom, and whether uncertainty supports the prose. A failed manipulation check can weaken an intervention interpretation, but post-treatment manipulation checks can themselves be affected by the intervention and require care.

For multi-item measures, inspect item composition, reverse coding, missing-item rules, reliability uncertainty, structural validity when claimed, and invariance across groups or time when score comparisons depend on it. Do not treat internal consistency alone as validity. A prior validation can rebut a concern only when the version, language, population, purpose, and scoring are sufficiently comparable.

Consider common-method or shared-informant inflation, proxy measures, task reliability, practice and order effects, ceiling or floor effects, and whether a statistically detectable change is presented as psychologically meaningful without support. Use APA JARS and COSMIN concepts only as applicable question sets, not numeric quality scores.

Evidence that can rebut a concern includes a time-stamped plan, a justified and disclosed deviation, exact scoring documentation, validation in a matched use, measurement-invariance evidence, balanced counterbalancing, outcome-independent exclusions, or claims narrowed to the observed measure. Do not infer p-hacking or misconduct, demand every robustness analysis, rescore raw responses, or generalize group measures to individual diagnosis.
