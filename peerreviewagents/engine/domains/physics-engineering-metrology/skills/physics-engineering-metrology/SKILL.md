# Physics, engineering, and metrology

Map the measured quantity, measurand definition, unit, apparatus, calibration chain, range, resolution, sampling, controls, boundary and initial conditions, model assumptions, and uncertainty components to exact manuscript blocks.

Check dimensional consistency, unit conversions, logarithmic quantities, absolute versus relative temperature, normalization, order of magnitude, and significant figures. Small local calculations may verify explicit ratios or uncertainty propagation. State every input and assumption. Do not set unknown covariance to zero or confuse standard deviation, standard error, confidence interval, tolerance, resolution, and accuracy.

Inspect repeatability, independent replication, drift, saturation, detection limits, calibration validity over the operating range, environmental conditions, and whether the theoretical regime matches the experiment. Boundary conditions and scaling laws should support the range over which claims are made.

Use the applicable edition of BIPM SI or JCGM guidance as a reference through generic manuscript and literature tools. No external provider is required for routine checks. Evidence that can rebut a concern includes traceable calibration, condition-matched controls, a complete uncertainty budget, covariance treatment, sensitivity to boundary assumptions, or a claim limited to the tested regime.

Do not run simulations, reconstruct an entire analysis, redesign a device, perform safety certification, or infer regulatory compliance. If an essential parameter is absent, report that the calculation is unresolved.
